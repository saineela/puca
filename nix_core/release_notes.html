Nix PUCA V6

Music you can queue, memory that answers for today instead of last year, and a pipeline that says what happened in words a person would use.

What changed, in plain words

  • Music is a real player now. The Music page appears when a music skill is installed: search, play, queue, playlists, lyrics and cover art in one place, on the dashboard, the wall panel or the phone.
  • A whole playlist is one action. Add a single track or a three-hour playlist; the queue keeps its place when you change pages, reload, or restart the dashboard, and every screen shows the same line-up.
  • Only one thing decides what plays next. The speaker reports what it is doing, the queue decides and starts the next track once, so two open tabs can no longer start the same song twice.
  • The speaker gets a second chance. If it refuses the stream, Nix retries once over the direct link, then says “the speaker wouldn’t take it — nothing’s playing. Want me to try again?” instead of showing the error.
  • Today’s answer, not last year’s. “What city do I live in?” returns the current city only; “where did I live before?” still finds the old one.
  • Phrases people actually use now work. “Put the optician in for Monday at 10am” books it, “the renewal is due March 4” is remembered, and “delete the note about my old address” asks first instead of guessing.
  • Fewer dead ends. More everyday ways of asking reach the right part of Nix, and turns that used to come back as “I couldn’t route that” now get a real answer.
  • It sounds like a person when it can’t help. A missing memory is “I don’t have that yet. Tell me and I’ll remember it.” — no knowledge bases, planners or error codes, and no claim that something was saved when it wasn’t.

For researchers: what changed under the hood

  • Routing. A deterministic turn expectation runs before a pipeline is chosen, and the routing ledger reports false escalation and false de-escalation separately, each with its own denominator. On the frozen 32-case replay, overall routing accuracy moved 75.0% → 87.5%, unroutable turns fell 43.8% → 9.4%, and escalation-required turns routed to Knowledge rose 61.1% → 83.3%; fast-path precision stayed flat at 92.9%, and router cost is unchanged within noise (p50 0.107 → 0.105 ms).
  • Personal recall and writes. Recall selection excludes superseded records unless the question carries a past frame; calendar writes gained deterministic “put X in for …” rules that defer on an unresolvable time; durable “X is due …” statements reach create_fact, and a conversational prediction can no longer demote a boundary decision to chat; delete_fact requires an explicit confirmation and fails closed with not_found / refused_too_broad.
  • Memory measurement. On the frozen 75 known / 25 unknown set, mean fact recall moved 12.67% → 57.33% (held-out 7.5% → 32.5%) and unknown-question honesty 88% → 100%. The same run still puts a full-transcript baseline at 81.3%, so no memory advantage is claimed; the frozen v1 scoring rule is unchanged, with the normalising v2 rule published separately.
  • Music queue as its own service. SQLite behind a service on port 49119 that advertises its address in the data directory: whole-playlist writes, revision-checked windowed reads, a token-bucket provider rate limit, background resolution with miss back-off, provider outages counted as outages rather than refusals, and playback that advances at most once per finished track.
  • Speaker hand-off. A refused hand-off is retried once on the direct stream and recorded as handoff.fallback = {url, first_error}, so a fallback is visible in the request log rather than looking like a normal start.
  • Reply wording. A deterministic voice_copy layer rewrites Core-authored failure and empty-result replies at Brain._finish without touching the model’s prompt or persona. On a 14-item abstention corpus, replies meeting all four criteria went 21.4% → 100%.
  • Measurement surface and docs. Read-only /api/system-model routes expose inference metrics, the privacy boundary, proactivity (off by default), context composition and turn-taking; the Documentation page lists every music, voice, panel and system-model route, and a test fails if the page drifts from the served routes.
  • Verification and limits. Current local runs: Core 797 passed / 9 skipped, Knowledge 502 passed. Raw logs and paired before/after tables are in docs/research/BENCHMARK_IMPROVEMENTS_2026-10-18.md. One playback variant still starts nothing until a device reports a fresh state — safe, but tracked as an open edge. V6 here is the product label: Luna V6 remains the source default and Casper V6 remains beta; no checkpoint is promoted by these notes.

Nix PUCA V5

  • OpenAI-compatible API conversations use isolated, namespaced thread IDs and do not inherit dashboard conversation history.
  • The API rejects structurally recognized OpenWebUI title, tag, and follow-up metadata-generation jobs before they reach Core or a conversation model.
  • Conversation browsing supports search and transcripts for dashboard and API-created chats.
  • Official Luna remains the source-configured default; Casper V5 and Casper V6 beta remain distinct selectable model choices when local artifacts are available.
  • Skills has a catalog install/status interface. The Web Search entry is explicitly not runnable; execution logic remains pending.
