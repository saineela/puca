Manifest component counts

The archived V2 snapshot metadata reports exactly 1,800 broad public-dialogue examples, 461 sampled project controls, 18 authored single-turn examples, 360 authored multi-turn trajectories, and 14 role controls, totaling 2,653. It also reports 223 context-bearing examples as a subset of the total, not an additional component. Source counts for the broad rows: DailyDialog 1,292; No Robots 185; UltraFeedback chosen-SFT 183; FineTome-100k 140. The metadata says no personal logs were included, 18 held-out benchmark prompts were checked, and there were zero exact overlaps at build time. It warns that semantic paraphrase overlap is not ruled out.

Evaluation caveats

Archived matched checks report the contextual V6 adapter at 4/9 single-turn and 1/5 multi-turn without injected context, and 6/9 + 2/5 with evaluator-injected context. The comparison baseline was reported at 5/9 + 1/5 isolated and 7/9 + 2/5 with injected context. Injected context was hand-authored evaluator input; it was not a full Core/Knowledge/Actions evaluation. These are archived experiment records, not new evaluation or proof of release quality. The V2 snapshot was later audited for a potentially over-broad sister-memory hint before a general positive turn. A separate corrected V3 snapshot is documented as untrained and unscored and is not the data recorded for the current adapter.

Local records: nix_knowledge/models/nixlm/luna-v6-contextual-v1-training-metadata.json, nix_knowledge/models/training_data/luna_targeted_v6_contextual_v2_sft.metadata.json, and PROJECT_HANDOFF.md (archived Luna section).

Dataset sources and rights status

  • DailyDialog — Li et al. (2017), ACL Anthology paper. Project source metadata records CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; review source terms before reuse or redistribution.
  • No Robots — Hugging Face dataset card. Project source metadata records CC BY-NC 4.0; verify exact source revision and terms.
  • UltraFeedback chosen SFT subset — Cui et al. (2023), paper and dataset card. Project snapshot records MIT for the source, but exact derivative/revision still needs verification.
  • FineTome-100k — dataset card. Archived notes say license/provenance is unresolved; treat as uncleared.
  • Project-authored controls and role examples are in-repository source records, not third-party citations. Dataset-generation/training steps are not authorized; this archive records history only.

Casper datasets & citations

  • DailyDialog — Li et al. (2017), ACL Anthology paper. Casper V5/V6 project metadata records CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
  • EmpatheticDialogues — Rashkin et al. (2019), ACL Anthology paper. Project dataset builders/manifests record CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, while an archived source-card note reports CC BY-NC 4.0. Verify exact source revision and terms before reuse or redistribution.
  • V5 builder excluded boilerplate, URLs, secrets, crisis details, and malformed turns. Its manifest reports seed 42 and 2,941/400 train/evaluation split. Generated dataset files and model weights are ignored local artifacts.
  • V6 dataset snapshots are separate records: initial SFT metadata gives 1,003 train / 250 eval rows, 53 curated rows, and six initial preference rows; the later policy-stage notes describe 288 SFT rows and 144 internally approved preference pairs. Do not add these stage-specific counts together as a single dataset.

Evaluation and promotion

Archived V6 reports record 7/8 on a policy-conditioned single-turn check, an earlier direct multi-turn 3/5, and the latest documented direct multi-turn 1/5. Small synthetic checks and training loss are not release-quality proof. V6 remains beta and is not default. V5 remains a distinct selectable fallback where its local artifacts are present.

Archive status & further notes

Luna Pro V1 research and artifacts are retired and preserved only for provenance. Its unresolved license/provenance and data issues are not permission to reuse its data or approach for any model. Earlier Casper, Luna, qwen35-humanlike, NixLM, and other adapters are experimental lineage, not additional current runtime selectors.

The broader local inventory and preservation notes are in PROJECT_HANDOFF.md, Luna archived plan/record, and PUCA V4 research tracks. These records identify source paths and reported history only; ignored model artifacts are not included in Git and current presence can vary between installations.

NIX-Modeldev is a provenance archive, not a training authorization, legal clearance, model card certification, or deployment-status dashboard.
